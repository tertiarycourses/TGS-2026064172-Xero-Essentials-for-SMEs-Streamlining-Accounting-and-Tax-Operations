# Xero Essentials for SMEs: Streamlining Accounting and Tax Operations

WSQ courseware for **TGS-2020505315** · Accounting and Tax System (ACC-ICT-4001-1.1).

## Learner materials

- root courseware files — trainer PPTX/PDF, Learner Guide and Lesson Plan
- `activities/` — ten progressive Xero SME activities with realistic CSV/XLSX mock data
- `LG-TGS-2020505315-Xero Essentials for SMEs - Streamlining Accounting and Tax Operations-13.0.md` — accessible Markdown mirror of the Learner Guide

## Design boundary

The slide deck is concept-led and visual. Detailed click-by-click procedures, evidence requirements and acceptance criteria live in the Learner Guide and activity packs.

## Scenario

All activities follow Lumina Homeware Pte Ltd, a fictional Singapore SME. All names and financial records are mock data for training only.
