Course code: TGS-2020505315  
TSC: Accounting and Tax System (ACC-ICT-4001-1.1)

## Legacy coverage floor

The 182-slide v12 trainer deck in `reference/` was used as the coverage floor. Its useful subject matter is retained and reorganised into a 125-slide, one-day, concept-led course:

- Xero organisation, financial and user-access settings
- chart of accounts, contacts, products/services and conversion balances
- bank accounts, statement imports, reconciliation and transfers
- quotes, invoices, receipts, credit notes and overdue receivables
- purchase orders, bills, payments, supplier credits and recurring transactions
- tracked and untracked inventory, adjustments and history
- Singapore GST coding, input/output tax controls and GST F5 review
- Profit and Loss, Balance Sheet, cash flow, AR/AP ageing and custom reports

Click-by-click procedures were deliberately moved from the deck into the Learner Guide and the ten self-contained activity folders.
