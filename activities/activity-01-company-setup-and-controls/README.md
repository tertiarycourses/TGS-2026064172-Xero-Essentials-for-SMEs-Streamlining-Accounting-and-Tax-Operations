# Activity 01: Set Up the SME Organisation and Control Profile

**Scenario:** Lumina Homeware Pte Ltd  
**Level:** Foundation · **Duration:** 25 min · **Alignment:** A1 · K1

## Business objective

Create a controlled Xero organisation profile for Lumina Homeware Pte Ltd.

## Files

- `Activity-01-Control-and-Evidence.xlsx` — intake, control checks, evidence and exception log
- `Activity-01-Xero-Import-Source.csv` — realistic mock source data; import only when the procedure calls for it

## Detailed step-by-step procedure

1. Open the activity workbook and read the Scenario and Control Matrix sheets before using Xero.
2. Sign in to the trainer-provided Xero demo or trial organisation; confirm that it contains mock data only.
3. Select the organisation name, open Settings, and record Organisation details, Financial settings, Invoice settings and Users in the Evidence Log.
4. Enter the legal name, customer-facing name, UEN, GST registration number, contact details and Singapore address from the workbook.
5. Set the financial year end to 31 December and confirm the accounting basis required by the scenario.
6. Confirm SGD as home currency; do not enable multi-currency until the irreversible-setting check is signed off.
7. Review GST settings against the 9% standard rate and quarterly filing scenario; do not mark any period as filed.
8. Create the proposed user-access matrix without inviting real external users; use trainer-provided dummy addresses only if instructed.
9. Capture screenshots of the company, financial-year and GST settings, excluding personal information.
10. Complete the Control Checks sheet and record any plan/UI difference in the Exception Log.

## Evidence to submit

- Completed control-and-evidence workbook
- Relevant Xero report(s) or screenshots without personal data
- Completed exception/decision log
- Reviewer sign-off

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Legal identity and financial year match the scenario
- [ ] GST profile is documented
- [ ] Home currency decision is approved
- [ ] Access roles follow least privilege

## Safety and privacy

Use only the trainer-provided training company and mock data. Do not upload real customer, supplier, employee, bank or tax data. Do not invite real external users.
