# Activity 04: Process the Quote-to-Cash Cycle

**Scenario:** Lumina Homeware Pte Ltd  
**Level:** Intermediate · **Duration:** 30 min · **Alignment:** A2 · K2

## Business objective

Process a quote, invoice, payment, customer credit note and overdue follow-up.

## Files

- `Activity-04-Control-and-Evidence.xlsx` — intake, control checks, evidence and exception log
- `Activity-04-Xero-Import-Source.csv` — realistic mock source data; import only when the procedure calls for it

## Detailed step-by-step procedure

1. Review the Sales Orders sheet and confirm customer, item, quantity, price, terms and GST treatment.
2. Create quote EST-1001 for Lion City Cafe Pte Ltd and verify that it does not post to the general ledger.
3. Convert the accepted quote to invoice INV-1001 without rekeying; confirm quantity, price and 9% GST.
4. Create an immediate-payment invoice for a walk-in sale, record the payment and reconcile it to the correct bank account.
5. Receive and apply the customer payment to INV-1001; verify that the unapplied amount is zero.
6. Create the approved customer credit for returned goods and apply it to the relevant open balance.
7. Preview the invoice branding theme and verify legal identity, GST details, terms and readable line information.
8. Run the customer transaction list and compare it with the Expected Postings sheet.
9. Run AR Ageing Summary as at month-end and identify the top two overdue balances.
10. Document the collection priority, owner, promised date and escalation trigger in the Action Log.

## Evidence to submit

- Completed control-and-evidence workbook
- Relevant Xero report(s) or screenshots without personal data
- Completed exception/decision log
- Reviewer sign-off

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Quote-to-invoice chain is intact
- [ ] Payment and credit are applied correctly
- [ ] GST is consistent with source documents
- [ ] AR ageing actions are specific and evidenced
