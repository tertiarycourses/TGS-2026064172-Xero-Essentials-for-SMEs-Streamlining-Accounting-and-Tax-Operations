# Activity 06: Control Inventory, Tracking Categories and History

**Scenario:** Lumina Homeware Pte Ltd  
**Level:** Intermediate · **Duration:** 25 min · **Alignment:** A2 · K2

## Business objective

Review inventory movements, tracking quality and History & Notes evidence.

## Files

- `Activity-06-Control-and-Evidence.xlsx` — intake, control checks, evidence and exception log
- `Activity-06-Xero-Import-Source.csv` — realistic mock source data; import only when the procedure calls for it

## Detailed step-by-step procedure

1. Review the Inventory Roll-forward sheet and confirm opening quantity and cost for each item.
2. Compare purchases, sales, returns and adjustments with the expected closing quantities.
3. Investigate negative stock, unusual adjustment dates and unsupported changes; do not post a plug adjustment.
4. Apply the approved tracking-category taxonomy to the sample transactions where the training plan supports it.
5. Run a transaction report filtered for blank tracking categories and record the exceptions.
6. Open the History & Notes report and filter for the activity period and transaction changes.
7. Trace one created, one edited and one voided/deleted example to its user and timestamp.
8. Assess whether the user role and activity are appropriate under the access matrix.
9. Document corrective actions and training needs in the Audit Review sheet.
10. Sign off the inventory and audit controls only when supporting evidence is complete.

## Evidence to submit

- Completed control-and-evidence workbook
- Relevant Xero report(s) or screenshots without personal data
- Completed exception/decision log
- Reviewer sign-off

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Inventory roll-forward reconciles
- [ ] Untracked items are resolved or logged
- [ ] History samples identify user and change
- [ ] Corrective actions have owner and due date

## Safety and privacy

Use only the trainer-provided training company and mock data. Do not upload real customer, supplier, employee, bank or tax data. Do not invite real external users.
