# Activity 08: Prepare the GST F5 Review Pack

**Scenario:** Lumina Homeware Pte Ltd  
**Level:** Advanced · **Duration:** 30 min · **Alignment:** A3 · K3 · K4

## Business objective

Reconcile GST ledgers and prepare a review pack for the quarterly GST return.

## Files

- `Activity-08-Control-and-Evidence.xlsx` — intake, control checks, evidence and exception log
- `Activity-08-Xero-Import-Source.csv` — realistic mock source data; import only when the procedure calls for it

## Detailed step-by-step procedure

1. Confirm the quarter, accounting basis and period lock in the GST Review workbook.
2. Run the GST F5 return and review Transactions by box number for the same period.
3. Reconcile taxable sales and purchases to the relevant P&L/ledger totals.
4. Reconcile the GST F5 return detail to Xero's single GST system account.
5. Investigate differences caused by dates, rounding, credits, journals or changes to prior periods.
6. Populate the F5 Control sheet from validated Xero report totals; do not manually override formulas.
7. Trace at least three sales and three purchase items from report to source document.
8. Review the exception log and confirm that every material item is corrected or escalated.
9. Record the preparer and independent reviewer sign-off.
10. Retain the review pack; filing on myTax Portal is outside this training activity.

## Evidence to submit

- Completed control-and-evidence workbook
- Relevant Xero report(s) or screenshots without personal data
- Completed exception/decision log
- Reviewer sign-off

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GST detail agrees to control accounts
- [ ] F5 control figures are formula-driven
- [ ] Sample evidence is traceable
- [ ] Preparer and reviewer sign-offs are complete

## Safety and privacy

Use only the trainer-provided training company and mock data. Do not upload real customer, supplier, employee, bank or tax data. Do not invite real external users.
